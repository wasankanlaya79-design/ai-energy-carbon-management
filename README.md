# AI Energy & Carbon Management System

MVP web application for organizational energy and carbon management.

## Included in this MVP
- Executive Dashboard
- Energy data entry and basic MJ conversion
- Carbon Scope 1–3 calculator
- EnPI calculator
- Significant Energy Use (SEU) view
- Energy conservation project ranking
- AI Energy Manager demo
- DEDE energy management 8-step compliance view
- Reports / Print to PDF
- Master conversion and emission factor placeholders

## Important
This version uses demo factors and demo data for interface validation only. Before real organizational use, replace demo conversion factors and emission factors with approved master data, including source, year, validity period, and version.
